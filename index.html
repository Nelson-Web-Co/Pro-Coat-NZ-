About Us

Excellence and Expertise

Pro Coat NZ is Nelson's trusted plastering and painting specialist. We invite you to discover our local business, where we transform our vision for striking and long-lasting plaster finishes — both interior and exterior — into reality.

With over 15 years of dedicated experience in the industry, we take immense pride in enhancing projects through our exceptional plastering and painting services, executed by our committed and skilled team.

Our extensive expertise covers exterior plastering and painting, interior finishing, crack repairs and new builds. Whether we apply traditional techniques or innovative methods, we are committed to delivering results that exceed your expectations.
